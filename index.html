How to Play

↔
Change Lane

Desktop: A/D or Left/Right Arrow · Mobile: swipe left/right

↑
Jump

Desktop: W, Up Arrow, or Space · Mobile: swipe up

↓
Slide

Desktop: S or Down Arrow · Mobile: swipe down

❚❚
Pause / Resume

Desktop: Escape or P · Mobile: tap the pause button

⚠
Avoid Office Obstacles

Jump over floor obstacles · Slide under security barriers

★
Collect Integrity Tokens

Consecutive tokens build a streak; higher streaks raise your score multiplier up to x4. Tokens do not restore Integrity.

✓
Near Miss

Clear an obstacle in your lane by a hair - jump or slide right past it for a bonus, no Integrity or multiplier risk.

Colliding with an obstacle costs Integrity and resets your streak and multiplier. Reach 0% Integrity and your run ends - keep to the three lanes and choose your moves carefully as you run through Corporate HQ.
